DOCUMENT: Microsoft Outlook Support

ISSUE: Unable to sign in to Outlook

1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Try signing in through outlook.office.com.
3. Check whether the account requires an MFA approval.
4. Check for password expiration or account lockout.
5. Contact IT support if access remains unavailable.

ISSUE: Outlook is not synchronizing emails

1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Check whether Outlook is in Work Offline mode.
3. Restart Outlook.
4. Check the mailbox status through Outlook on the web.
5. Contact IT support if synchronization does not resume.

SECURITY:
Never share passwords or MFA authentication codes with anyone.
