DOCUMENT: Corporate VPN Support

ISSUE: Unable to connect to corporate VPN

1. Verify that the internet connection is working.
2. Confirm that the approved company VPN application is installed.
3. Restart the VPN application.
4. Check whether the VPN service is experiencing an outage.
5. Contact IT support if the connection continues to fail.

SECURITY:
Never share VPN passwords or authentication codes.
Only use company-approved VPN applications.
